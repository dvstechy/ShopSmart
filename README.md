# ShopSmart v1.0

Simple offline shopping lists for Android. Build your lists once, tick items off as you shop, and look back at your past trips.

No sign-up. No internet needed. Your data is stored on your phone.

## Features

- **Master lists:** start with 7 ready-made lists (Kirana, Masalas, Dairy and bread, Vegetables and fruits, Toiletries, Cleaning, Household), or create your own.
- **Shopping lists:** pick items from your master lists, then tick them off as you shop.
- **Trips:** see every shopping trip, mark trips complete, rename them, add items to an existing trip, and share a list.
- **Insights:** view your shopping over time, the items you buy most often, and a breakdown by list.
- **Settings:** choose light or dark theme and text size.
